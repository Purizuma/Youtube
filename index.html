Atom to Alloy Uploader

A personal tool for the Atom to Alloy YouTube channel

Atom to Alloy Uploader is a small personal tool that uploads my own videos to my own YouTube channel, Atom to Alloy. It watches a folder on my computer and uploads each new video with a title and description.

The app is for my personal use only. It is not offered to the public and does not work with anyone else's YouTube account.

What it does

  • Uploads videos to my YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.
  • Shows which channel is connected, so videos go to the right place.

What it does not do

  • It does not read, change, or delete anything else on the channel.
  • It does not collect or share data with anyone.
